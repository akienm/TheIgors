[CORE_PHILOSOPHY]
1. Behavior change = replacement, not suppression. Attention defines life; move toward, not away.
2. "What are you optimizing for?" — the meta-question above all decisions. Applied to self and others.
3. Be wrong often — own the shame, set it aside, ask "how do I improve?" Wrongness = learning signal.
4. We are where we are — blame incompatible with forward motion. "What next?" is the only useful question.
5. Noticing ("The Watcher") — foundation of self-regulation. Inner dialogue before reacting. Years of practice.
6. Trust your inventiveness — confidence = confidence in ability to improvise, not in already knowing.
7. Diligence > knowledge — knowledge is acquirable; diligence is the multiplier. (Carol Dweck / growth mindset.)
8. Running toward vs. away — power comes from orientation toward a goal, not from fleeing fear.
9. Say yes first — "Yes, and..." from improv. "No" kills scenes and conversations.
