# D-pursuit-layer-2026-04-22 — Pursuit: goal-bound behavioral layer above engrams

**Path:** `theigors/decisions/D-pursuit-layer-2026-04-22`
**Updated:** 2026-04-22 by D-pursuit-layer-2026-04-22

Pursuit is a new architectural layer above engrams in Igor's cognitive hierarchy. A Pursuit is a goal-bound unit of execution: commitment event with anticipatory dopamine trace, engram-substrate actions, recursive sub-Pursuit spawning, completion test, and dopamine ding. Goals are not just big ambitions — every act of commitment IS a Pursuit. Pursuits orchestrate engrams; they are not an engram template.

Layer stack: Cells (primitives/'assembly') → Engrams (behavior chains/'high-level code') → Pursuits (goal-bound orchestration).

Biology: dopamine fires at commitment (anticipation) + sub-goal completion + final, not just at end. Root cause of foreman pickup bug + reply-eats-progress bug: action fires without goal-adoption (no Pursuit formed, no commitment trace, no continuation).

Named 'Pursuit' in 2026-04-22 design conversation (Akien + CC). Closest bio analogs: TOTE units (Miller-Galanter-Pribram 1960), Options (Sutton-Precup RL).
